🧾 Signing up — what really happens

Follow your sign-up form as it travels through the app. Endpoint: POST /api/auth/register
📝
STEP 1 — YOU
You fill the form and hit "Sign Up"
Your browser packs your details into a little JSON parcel and mails it to the app.
fullNameemailusernamepassworddepartment
🚪
STEP 2 — THE SECURITY GATE
The guard waves it straight through
Sign-up is a public door — you obviously don't need to be logged in to make an account. So no pass is checked here.
🔎
STEP 3 — THE FRONT DESK (controller)
"Is your form filled in correctly?"
Every field must be filled, and the email must look like a real email. If not, it's handed back to you unchanged.
Also: every new signup starts as a normal EMPLOYEE (not an admin).
✗ blank field or bad email → 400 Bad Request
🙅
STEP 4 — THE MEMBERS LIST (service)
"Is this username or email already taken?"
The app checks the existing members. If someone already uses that username or email, you're stopped right here.
✗ already exists → 409 Conflict
🔒
STEP 5 — THE SHREDDER (password hashing)
Your password gets scrambled, forever
The app never keeps your real password. It scrambles it into a fingerprint that can't be un-scrambled. Only the fingerprint is saved.
"secret123" ➜ $2a$10$k7Q…9fD (bcrypt hash)
💾
STEP 6 — THE DATABASE
Your account is written down
A new row is saved in the users table (name, email, username, the scrambled password, role, department). You now officially exist, with your own ID.
🎟️
STEP 7 — THE TICKET BOOTH
You're handed two tickets, so you don't log in again
Access ticket (JWT): a short-lived pass you show on every future request.
Refresh ticket: a long-lived cookie the browser keeps, to quietly get fresh passes later. It's HttpOnly, so scripts and hackers can't read it.
✅
STEP 8 — DONE
Registered AND already logged in
The app replies 201 Created. The response carries your access ticket + who you are; the refresh ticket rides along as a cookie. No separate login needed.
201 Created + access token + refresh cookie
